## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and engineering managers screening software-developer internship and junior candidates in India and remote; hackathon and club peers; the occasional founder looking for a builder. They arrive from a resume link (veerbhadra-mahant.me) and give the page well under a minute before deciding to open a project repository or write.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Veerbhadra Mahant, a third-year B.E. Information Technology student at PCCOE Pune who ships full-stack and AI products. The site must make a reader believe, within seconds, that this person builds real systems with measurable results (load tests, test counts, endpoint counts), wins hackathons, and leads a 50-member technical team. Success: the reader opens a project repository or sends an email.

## Positioning

A builder whose projects carry numbers: 1,000 concurrent calls at p95 33 ms with zero errors, 54 endpoints over an 11-table row-level-security schema, 170+ unit tests with CI end-to-end, a live vessel relay that costs nothing when idle. Most student portfolios list tools; this one lists outcomes.
